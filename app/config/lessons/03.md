## What is a Major Chord?

A Major Chord is a group of three notes played together. It has a "happy" and "bright" sound. This group of three notes is called a **Triad**.

## How to build a Major Chord?

To build a Major Chord, you use the Root note and two specific intervals:

1. **Root:** The starting note.
2. **Major Third:** 4 half steps (4 frets) above the root.
3. **Perfect Fifth:** 7 half steps (7 frets) above the root.

## Examples

### 1. C Major

- **Root:** C
- **Major Third:** E
- **Perfect Fifth:** G
- **Notes:** C, E, G

### 2. G Major

- **Root:** G
- **Major Third:** B
- **Perfect Fifth:** D
- **Notes:** G, B, D

### 3. A Major

- **Root:** A
- **Major Third:** C#
- **Perfect Fifth:** E
- **Notes:** A, C#, E
