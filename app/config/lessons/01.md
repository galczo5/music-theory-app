## The 12 Notes

In music, there are **12 different notes**. After the 12th note, the names repeat again.

The notes are:
**A, A#, B, C, C#, D, D#, E, F, F#, G, G#**

- The symbol **#** is called a **Sharp**.
- **Important:** There is no sharp between **B and C** and no sharp between **E and F**.

---

## Half Steps and Whole Steps

On the guitar, we measure the distance between notes using "steps."

### 1. Half Step (Semitone)

- The smallest distance in music.
- On the guitar, moving **1 fret** equals a Half Step.
- Example: Moving from the 1st fret to the 2nd fret.

### 2. Whole Step (Whole Tone)

- A Whole Step equals **two Half Steps**.
- On the guitar, moving **2 frets** equals a Whole Step.
- Example: Moving from the 1st fret to the 3rd fret.
