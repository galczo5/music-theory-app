## 7th Chords

A 7th chord adds a fourth note to the basic triad. This extra note is the 7th interval from the root.

### Major 7th (maj7)

- **Structure:** Root + Major 3rd + Perfect 5th + Major 7th.
- **Example (Cmaj7):** C, E, G, B.

### Minor 7th (m7)

- **Structure:** Root + Minor 3rd + Perfect 5th + Minor 7th.
- **Example (Am7):** A, C, E, G.

---

## 9th Chords

A 9th chord adds a fifth note to the 7th chord. On the guitar, we often skip the Perfect 5th to make these easier to play.

### Major 9th (maj9)

- **Structure:** Major 7th chord + Major 9th.
- **Example (Cmaj9):** C, E, G, B, D.

### Minor 9th (m9)

- **Structure:** Minor 7th chord + Major 9th.
- **Example (Am9):** A, C, E, G, B.
