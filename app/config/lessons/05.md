## 1. Augmented Chords (aug)

An Augmented chord has a "mysterious" or "unsettled" sound.

### How to build it:

- **Root:** The starting note.
- **Major Third:** 4 half steps above the root.
- **Augmented Fifth:** 8 half steps above the root.

### Examples:

- **C Augmented (Caug):** C, E, G#
- **G Augmented (Gaug):** G, B, D#
- **D Augmented (Daug):** D, F#, A#

---

## 2. Diminished Chords (dim)

A Diminished chord has a "tense" or "dark" sound. It is often used to create suspense.

### How to build it:

- **Root:** The starting note.
- **Minor Third:** 3 half steps above the root.
- **Diminished Fifth:** 6 half steps above the root.

### Examples:

- **C Diminished (Cdim):** C, Eb, Gb
- **G Diminished (Gdim):** G, Bb, Db
- **B Diminished (Bdim):** B, D, F

---

**Tip:** Augmented chords use a "raised" 5th, while Diminished chords use a "lowered" 3rd and 5th.
