## What is a Minor Chord?
A Minor Chord is a group of three notes played together. It usually has a "sad" or "dark" sound. Like the Major chord, it is also a **Triad**.

## How to build a Minor Chord?
To build a Minor Chord, you use the Root note and these two intervals:
1. **Root:** The starting note.
2. **Minor Third:** 3 half steps (3 frets) above the root.
3. **Perfect Fifth:** 7 half steps (7 frets) above the root.

---

## Examples

### 1. A Minor (Am)
* **Root:** A
* **Minor Third:** C
* **Perfect Fifth:** E
* **Notes:** A, C, E

### 2. E Minor (Em)
* **Root:** E
* **Minor Third:** G
* **Perfect Fifth:** B
* **Notes:** E, G, B

### 3. D Minor (Dm)
* **Root:** D
* **Minor Third:** F
* **Perfect Fifth:** A
* **Notes:** D, F, A

---

**Tip:** Compare the Minor Third to the Major Third from the previous lesson. The Minor Third is 1 fret lower.
