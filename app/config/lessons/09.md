## Step-by-Step Building
To build a scale, we use a specific pattern of **Whole Steps (W)** and **Half Steps (H)**. 

As a reminder:
* **W (Whole Step):** 2 frets on the guitar.
* **H (Half Step):** 1 fret on the guitar.

## The 7 Mode Formulas
Each mode has its own unique "DNA" or pattern:

1.  **Ionian:** W - W - H - W - W - W - H
2.  **Dorian:** W - H - W - W - W - H - W
3.  **Phrygian:** H - W - W - W - H - W - W
4.  **Lydian:** W - W - W - H - W - W - H
5.  **Mixolydian:** W - W - H - W - W - H - W
6.  **Aeolian:** W - H - W - W - H - W - W
7.  **Locrian:** H - W - W - H - W - W - W

---

## The Secret: Shifting the Pattern
Notice something interesting? All these modes use the **same sequence of steps**, but they start at a different position. 

If you take the Ionian pattern (W-W-H-W-W-W-H) and move the first "W" to the very end, you get the Dorian pattern. This is called **shifting**.

**This means:**
* All 7 modes belong to the same "family."
* They use the same notes, but because they start on a different note, they create a different mood.

---

## Example: The C Major Family
All these scales use only natural notes (no sharps or flats):
* **C Ionian:** Starts on **C** (C-D-E-F-G-A-B)
* **D Dorian:** Starts on **D** (D-E-F-G-A-B-C)
* **E Phrygian:** Starts on **E** (E-F-G-A-B-C-D)
* ...and so on.

**Tip:** Even though the notes are the same, the "home" note (the root) changes. That is what changes the sound!
