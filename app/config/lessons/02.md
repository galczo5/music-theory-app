## What is an Interval?
An interval is the **distance** between two musical notes. Knowing intervals helps you understand how melodies and chords are built.

## Interval Names and Half Steps
Each interval has a specific name based on the number of half steps:

* **Perfect Unison:** 0 half steps
* **Minor Second:** 1 half step
* **Major Second:** 2 half steps
* **Minor Third:** 3 half steps
* **Major Third:** 4 half steps
* **Perfect Fourth:** 5 half steps
* **Tritone:** 6 half steps
* **Perfect Fifth:** 7 half steps
* **Minor Sixth:** 8 half steps
* **Major Sixth:** 9 half steps
* **Minor Seventh:** 10 half steps
* **Major Seventh:** 11 half steps
* **Octave:** 12 half steps

---

**Tip for Guitarists:** 1 half step = 1 fret. 
2 half steps = 2 frets.
