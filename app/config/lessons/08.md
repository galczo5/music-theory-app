## What are Modes?

Modes are types of scales. There are seven main modes, and each one starts on a different note of the Major scale. Each mode has a unique "flavor" or mood.

All of these basic modes contain **7 notes**.

## The 7 Modes and Their Sounds

### 1. Ionian

- **Sound:** Happy and bright. This is the standard Major scale.
- **Notes:** 7 notes.

### 2. Dorian

- **Sound:** Cool and jazzy. It is a minor-type scale but sounds a bit brighter.
- **Notes:** 7 notes.

### 3. Phrygian

- **Sound:** Dark and exotic. It has a "Spanish" or Middle-Eastern feel.
- **Notes:** 7 notes.

### 4. Lydian

- **Sound:** Dreamy and "spacey." It is often used in film music.
- **Notes:** 7 notes.

### 5. Mixolydian

- **Sound:** Bluesy and rock-and-roll. It sounds like a Major scale with a slight twist.
- **Notes:** 7 notes.

### 6. Aeolian

- **Sound:** Sad and emotional. This is the standard Natural Minor scale.
- **Notes:** 7 notes.

### 7. Locrian

- **Sound:** Very tense and unstable. It is the darkest-sounding mode.
- **Notes:** 7 notes.

---

**Tip:** Think of modes as different "colors" you can use to paint your music.
